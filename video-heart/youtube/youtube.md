## Description
On Monday night, CD Tenerife forward Jesús de Miguel, 30, suffered cardiac arrest after a goalkeeper's knee struck his chest during Tenerife vs Andorra. He died the next day. Our thoughts are with his family, his teammates and everyone at CD Tenerife.

The exact cause is still under investigation. Doctors have named commotio cordis as the leading hypothesis while the autopsy is pending. This video explains that mechanism: how a blow to the chest at the wrong millisecond of the heartbeat can trigger a fatal rhythm, and why an AED within minutes is the best chance of survival.

Commotio cordis is rare but real. An impact to the chest, from a ball, a knee or an elbow, lands in a 10–40 millisecond window of the heartbeat and triggers ventricular fibrillation. No broken bones. No structural damage. Just electricity going wrong.
