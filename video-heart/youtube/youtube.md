## Title
How a Single Hit Can Stop a Heart: The Physics of Commotio Cordis

## Description
A healthy heart. One blow to the chest. And in a 20-millisecond window, the heart's electrical rhythm collapses.

This is commotio cordis: a rare but real phenomenon where an impact to the chest, from a ball, a knee or an elbow, lands at exactly the wrong moment in the heartbeat and triggers ventricular fibrillation. No broken bones. No structural damage. Just electricity going wrong.

In this video:
• How the heart works as an electrical pump
• The vulnerable window hidden in every heartbeat
• Why the timing of a hit matters more than its strength
• Why the first few minutes decide survival, and what an AED does
