## Descrição
In about sixty seconds, a Learjet 60 went from 35,000 feet inside a building thunderstorm to under 9,000 feet, descending faster than 32,000 feet per minute.

This video doesn't tell you the cause. The investigation is still open. What it does is walk through the physics that every flight at high altitude depends on: lift, air density, icing, the coffin corner, and what happens when a jet passes its maximum speed.

Everything you see is real-time 3D animation built entirely in code.
